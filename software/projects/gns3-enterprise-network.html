GNS3 Enterprise Network

Multi-Site Topology Design

Problem

Enterprise networks require careful planning of routing protocols, VLAN segmentation, and firewall policies across multiple sites — difficult to test without a lab environment.

Solution

Designed and deployed a multi-site enterprise network in GNS3 with OSPF area configuration, inter-VLAN routing, ACL-based firewall rules, and simulated WAN links between branch offices.
