Who I Am

I'm Cristian Diaz, a Computer Science and Information Security student at John Jay College. I combine software engineering with a deep interest in cybersecurity — building secure systems and breaking them apart to understand how they fail.

How I Got Into Tech

My path started in civil engineering at Hostos Community College. I discovered programming at LaGuardia and realized I wanted to build software that protects people and data, not just infrastructure blueprints.

Army Background

I serve in the Army National Guard as an Infantry Soldier. The military taught me discipline, teamwork under pressure, and attention to detail — skills I apply directly to security operations and software quality.

Transition Into Cybersecurity

Through SEED labs, CTF platforms, and hands-on cloud security projects, I moved from writing code to thinking like an attacker and defender. I'm pursuing Security+ and building tools like Sentinel-Lite to scan AWS environments for misconfigurations.

Career Goals

I aim to work as a security-minded software engineer — building resilient backends, automating security workflows, and contributing to open-source security tooling.
